# Dissertation Positioning

## Central Position
This dissertation is a methodological exploration, not a clinically validated multimodal breast cancer study.

## Safe Claim
The work evaluates whether synthetic label-aligned pairing of independent image and tabular breast cancer datasets can serve as an exploratory framework for testing multimodal fusion strategies when true matched multimodal datasets are unavailable.

## Required Limitation Language
- The tabular and image datasets are independent.
- No patient-level correspondence exists between the two datasets.
- Pairing is artificial and based only on class labels.
- Observed fusion performance does not imply real clinical multimodal validity.
- Results should be interpreted as exploratory and methodological.

## Contribution Framing
- defensible patient-level image baseline on BreaKHis
- preserved and documented tabular baseline from existing published work
- controlled synthetic pairing experiments
- comparison of early fusion, late fusion, and control pairings
- honest analysis of where the synthetic framework is and is not useful
